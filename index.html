SURVIVAL GUIDE: HEALING WHILE PARENTING

You're not broken. The system is. It's time to reclaim yourself.

This zine is a free resource for parents healing from their own childhoods while raising their kids. It was made by someone who gets it. It's for those of us building the plane while we're flying it.

It's free. Pass it on.

# Faceless Reclaimation

For the stories that survived in fragments.

You are not required to make your life look clean before it becomes true.

Some stories arrive broken open because that is how they had to survive. Some language has to be recovered from pressure, paperwork, silence, shame, survival, and everything that tried to name the story from the outside.

Faceless Reclaimation is a public-facing artifact and language project for reclaiming story, naming pressure, and turning lived fragments into usable forms.

### Core line

The work is not to perform coherence for people who only believed the wound when it looked organized.

The work is to recover the right to name what happened without letting the naming become another cage.

Why Support This Work?

The "Healing While Parenting" zine is completely free because we believe that breaking cycles shouldn't cost money. But creating quality resources, maintaining this website, and developing new materials takes time and resources.

Your support helps us:

  • Keep all resources completely free and accessible
  • Create new zines for different aspects of recovery and healing
  • Maintain and improve this website
  • Print physical copies for distribution in communities that need them most
  • Develop interactive tools and resources

Other Ways to Support

Share the Resource: The most powerful way to support this work is to share it. Post the link on social media, send it to friends, or leave printed copies in places where parents might find them.

Leave Feedback: Let us know how the zine helped you or what other resources you'd like to see. Your input shapes what we create next.

Print and Distribute: Download the PDF, print copies, and leave them in doctors' offices, libraries, community centers, or anywhere parents gather.

What's Next?

This is just the beginning. We're working on additional zines in the Faceless Reclamation series, including:

  • Reclaiming Anger: Healthy ways to process and express anger
  • Reclaiming Rest: Permission to rest without guilt
  • Reclaiming Boundaries: Setting limits that protect your energy
  • Reclaiming Joy: Finding lightness in the midst of healing

Your support makes all of this possible.

Transparency

We believe in being transparent about how donations are used. Funds go directly toward:

  • Website hosting and maintenance
  • Design and illustration costs
  • Printing costs for community distribution
  • Time spent creating and refining content

This is a grassroots effort. Every dollar is used thoughtfully and with intention.

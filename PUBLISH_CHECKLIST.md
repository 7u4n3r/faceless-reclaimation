# PUBLISH_CHECKLIST.md

## Purpose

Pre-publish checklist for Faceless Reclaimation public surfaces.

Use this before turning `public/INDEX_COPY.md` or any draft material into a live page, zine hub, product doorway, or public artifact surface.

## Current public copy source

`public/INDEX_COPY.md`

## Publish posture

Faceless Reclaimation may publish as the Reclaimation-facing public body.

It may carry:

- story
- artifact energy
- zine material
- lived-language fragments
- public-facing Reclaimation copy
- digital offering doorway language

It should not become:

- Perspection governance
- Trinity doctrine
- System Analysis Reference
- ABLE-KY county navigation
- the whole ecosystem

## Pre-publish checklist

Before publishing, confirm:

- [ ] The page identifies itself as Faceless Reclaimation.
- [ ] The page does not claim to be Perspection.
- [ ] The page does not claim to be the Trinity Framework.
- [ ] The page does not present the entire ecosystem as one project.
- [ ] Related projects are labeled as related, not absorbed.
- [ ] The copy does not over-explain the full ecosystem.
- [ ] The copy keeps a public-facing artifact/story/Reclaimation posture.
- [ ] Any product links are intentional and working.
- [ ] Any Gumroad, Notion, or external links are checked before publishing.
- [ ] No private/legal/evidence material is accidentally included.
- [ ] No personal identifiers are included unless intentionally public.
- [ ] The page can stand alone for a stranger who has never seen the ecosystem.

## Voice check

The page may be:

- direct
- raw
- poetic
- artifact-like
- grounded
- emotionally alive
- public-facing

The page should avoid becoming:

- institutional
- over-explained
- governance-heavy
- method-heavy
- legal-claim-heavy
- a full autobiography
- a total ecosystem manifesto

## Boundary check

Allowed line:

> Faceless Reclaimation is a public-facing artifact and language project for reclaiming story, naming pressure, and turning lived fragments into usable forms.

Avoid lines like:

> Faceless Reclaimation is Perspection.
> Faceless Reclaimation is the Trinity Framework.
> Faceless Reclaimation contains the entire system.
> Faceless Reclaimation is legal evidence.

## Publish-safe description

Faceless Reclaimation is a public-facing artifact and language project for reclaiming story, naming pressure, and turning lived fragments into usable forms.

## Ultra-short description

For the stories that survived in fragments.

## Final pass questions

Before publishing, ask:

1. Does this page know what it is?
2. Does this page know what it is not?
3. Does it give the reader an entry point without swallowing the whole ecosystem?
4. Is anything private, legal-sensitive, or unsorted accidentally exposed?
5. Are links intentional and working?

## Operating rule

Faceless Reclaimation carries the public story body.

It does not become governance.
It does not become Trinity doctrine.
It does not absorb every tool.

## Source fragments preserved for rewrite

### Taking back your story

You are standing in the fire.

An experience, a feeling, or a force is consuming the whole frame. It feels total, absolute, and real.

That raw encounter is the lived fire.

It is the starting point, but it is not the whole story.

### The core problem

A person can fuse with the first story the fire tells.

The body reacts.
The mind names the reaction.
The world starts treating that name like truth.

That is how outside forces become faceless. They do not always need a single visible author. They move through fear, conditioning, shame, paperwork, memory, habit, and survival pressure.

Faceless Reclaimation exists where the story starts to come back into your own hands.

### Reclaimation-facing language

The work is not to perform coherence for people who only believed the wound when it looked organized.

The work is to recover the right to name what happened without letting the naming become another cage.
